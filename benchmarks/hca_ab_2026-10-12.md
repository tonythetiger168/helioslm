## Reading

1. **Pure chunk-pooled HCA collapses to chance at every ratio** — even
   8× mean-pooling destroys the exact token this probe retrieves. The
   breakpoint for exact recall is not gradual; it is immediate.
2. **Sparse top-k rescue is the mechanism that restores exact recall**:
   rescue@4 lifts 8× to 0.93 (above dense, 0.91 — the rescued token
   bypasses softmax dilution over 512 dense positions) and 128× to
   0.77–0.81.
3. **The rescue-vs-ratio degradation (0.94 → 0.77 as compression goes
   8× → 128×) is the quality-breakpoint curve**: at higher compression
   the rescue competes against more pooled-slot probability mass, and
   the indexer scores a larger compressed region.

## Why constructive (identity-wired) weights

MLA's absorbed cache is lossless up to fp reassociation (the repo suite
verifies absorbed == expanded at atol 1e-4), so plain full causal
attention is the honest MLA-quality reference. Identity-wiring every
arm (q = k = v = x, out = identity, indexer = identity) isolates the
ATTENTION PATTERN's information capacity from training dynamics: same
geometry, same weights, same seeds — only the attention structure
differs. A trained-circuit variant (indexer distillation via
`MLA.indexer_distill_loss` machinery + end-to-end passkey training) is
the documented follow-up, deliberately out of scope here (CPU budget).
