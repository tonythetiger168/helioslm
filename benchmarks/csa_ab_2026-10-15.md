## Reading

1. **Mean-pooled CSA collapses to chance at 4× and 16×, like pure HCA.**
2. **Selection is not the bottleneck.** The indexer puts the chunk that
   holds the queried token into the top-k in 86–100% of trials, yet
   accuracy stays at chance.
3. **The compressor is.** In this probe the planted token beats the
   query's own token by only boost/√2 ≈ 1.13×. Mean-pooling it with
   `ratio − 1` other tokens divides that lead by the ratio
   (≈ 0.28× at 4×), so the selected entry never out-scores the query
   itself (0/200 trials for every arm at L=512, checked during
   development; not part of the artifact).
4. **Implication for the roadmap:** an exact-recall CSA needs either a
   learned compressor (V4 uses one; this reference mean-pools — a
   recorded deviation) or an exact-token path, which is what
   HighCompressionAttention's rescue provides (`hca_ab_2026-10-12`:
   0.94 at 8×). The next Phase B step for `v4_csa` is a learned
   compressor, measured on this same probe.

## Caveats

Identity-wired weights measure what the attention pattern can carry,
not what training finds. n = 200 per cell.
